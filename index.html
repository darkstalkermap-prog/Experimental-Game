Raising the castles…

CASTLE WARS

Four lords. Four castles. Armies that never stop marching.
You are the lord of the blue castle. Every castle sends a fresh army marching out every 30 seconds, for as long as it stands. Earn gold from kills and spend it on soldiers, castle upgrades and siege engines; pick a hero class; and choose who garrisons your castle and who marches with you. Break their gates with swords or rams, breach their walls with catapults and destroy their keep. Cut down an engine's crew and it is yours for the taking. Conquest is the main mode: the last castle and hero standing wins. In King of the Hill the same war has a hill in the middle of the map, and the house that holds it long enough wins outright.

      Options

      Graphics
      Auto picks a level for your machine and lowers the resolution a little whenever the frame rate drops. Low turns off shadows and grass for the smoothest play; Ultra adds the sharpest shadows and ambient occlusion.
      Alerts: callouts and sounds for attacks, waves and low health, and a summary when you look away. Tips: a hint line now and then. Battle Decrees: after each battle, one or two random rule changes are rolled for the next (you can reroll them or play a classic battle).
