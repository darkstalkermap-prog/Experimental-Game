Raising the castles…

CASTLE WARS

Four lords. Four castles. Armies that never stop marching.
You are the lord of the blue castle. Every castle sends a fresh army marching out every 30 seconds, for as long as it stands. Earn gold from kills and spend it on soldiers, castle upgrades and siege engines; pick a hero class; and choose who garrisons your castle and who marches with you. Break their gates with swords or rams, breach their walls with catapults and destroy their keep. Cut down an engine's crew and it is yours for the taking. The last castle and hero standing wins.
